## [HIGH] Strategy Autotuning

Automatically adjust entry filters using analytics data.

Goals

* Detect indicators that eliminate too many candidates
* Adjust thresholds dynamically
* Adapt strategy to market regime

Possible adjustments

* ADX threshold
* MACD sensitivity
* VWAP tolerance
* SMA distance threshold
* score threshold

Example rule
IF VWAP eliminates >80% of candidates
THEN relax VWAP tolerance slightly

Future

* Bayesian parameter optimization

## [HIGH] Time-of-Day Weighting

Analytics indicates entries perform differently by hour.

Example observation
09:00  ~37% winrate
10:00  ~25%
11:00  ~28%
12:00  ~32%
13:00  ~41%   <-- strongest
14:00  ~21%
15:00  ~22%

Goal
Weight entry confidence by time-of-day.

Example
entry_score *= hour_weight

Possible weights
09:00   1.1
10:00   0.8
11:00   0.9
12:00   1.0
13:00   1.2
14:00   0.7
15:00   0.7
